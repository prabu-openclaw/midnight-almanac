The Midnight Almanac

Recorded nightly. Correct at midnight. No refunds after dawn.
—
— ✦ —
How It Is Made: Every entry is drawn from this book's own pages by the date alone. The same day yields the same reading for every reader, anywhere; tomorrow's will not resemble today's, and no one — not even the printer — has looked ahead. The numbers are fixed, the weather is not, and the creature is always hungry. Corrections are received in good faith and printed never. If your reading seems aimed at you personally, remember that a horoscope is only a stranger's grocery list read aloud.
